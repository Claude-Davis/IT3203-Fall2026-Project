Evolution of the Domain Naming System

Though the Domain Naming System got its start in the early 1980s, it has, like most technology, continued to advance over the years to resolve challenges and meet changing user needs. Previous slides mentioned methods of encryption used to protect client’s privacy when searching DNS servers. The innovation of digital data encryption remains relatively new but, as mentioned, several methods exist to protect user privacy. The DNS is also noted for having become more dynamic over the years. Though servers previously followed a set path for each user, they now provide different responses based on factors unique to the client. As businesses evolve and business workloads increase, technology is made to handle more tasks at higher speeds and efficiency. The DNS is no exception. Enterprises often utilize multiple authoritative DNS servers to reduce the impact if a server fails. Having these back ups also eases the difficulty of rebuilding a server, if needed.
